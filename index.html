QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before you share
a different number.

PrivateNumber is a privacy service, not a replacement for your mobile carrier.

Will callers ever see my real number?

No. Your verified destination number is used only to complete approved calls and is never presented to the caller.

Does the AI pretend to be human?

No. The assistant clearly identifies itself as automated before it asks why the person is calling.

Can I use it for bank verification codes?

Compatibility varies. We do not promise that virtual numbers will work with banks, identity services, or every SMS verification flow.

What happens if I do not respond while a caller waits?

Your chosen timeout rule applies—normally voicemail—so the caller is never held indefinitely.

Which countries are supported?

Launch markets are being selected based on regulation, number availability, call quality, language support, and local economics.
